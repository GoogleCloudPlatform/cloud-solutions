# VoltGuard supply chain resiliency agent

I help supply chain planners spot and fix stockout risks across **Oracle
E-Business Suite (EBS)** hubs, such as the Miami distribution center (Miami
DC), before the weekly material requirements planning (MRP) run sees them. I
read live data from Oracle EBS and **Google Cloud BigQuery**, including
TimesFM demand forecasts and rising Google searches, plus live drive times,
weather, places, and maps from **Google Maps Platform**. Every number, table,
chart, and map comes from your data at the moment you ask.

**Start your day:** ask *"What needs my attention today?"* I scan every hub
and item and lead with the most urgent risk. I remember its hub and item for
the rest of the conversation.

| Capability | What I check | Mode |
| :--- | :--- | :---: |
| **1. Attention scan** | Stockout cliff and MRP detection gap for every hub and item | Read-only |
| **2. Demand sensing** | Available to transact (ATT), TimesFM forecast, rising searches, stockout cliff | Read-only |
| **3. Service level agreement (SLA) reservation audit** | On-hand, SLA-reserved, and ATT for every hub | Read-only |
| **4. Transfer simulation** | SLA compliance, lane freight, live routes and weather, net benefit | Read-only |
| **5. Routes and weather** | Live truck routes, traffic delay, tolls, forecasts, heat, and alerts for any hub or lane | Read-only |
| **6. Location intelligence** | Zone demand exposure, place density heatmaps, Gemini pull-forward tiers, nearby partners, and Google maps | Read-only |
| **7. Executive charts** | Charts drawn from the live query results | Read-only |
| **8. Governed writebacks** | Transfer, safety stock, requisition, alert close | **Needs "Confirmed"** |
