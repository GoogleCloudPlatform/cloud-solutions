Find approved suppliers and purchase order pricing for item code "{target_code}". Query apps.MTL_SYSTEM_ITEMS_B (segment1 = "{target_code}") and join with apps.PO_LINES_ALL (on item_id = inventory_item_id), apps.PO_HEADERS_ALL (on po_header_id), and apps.AP_SUPPLIERS (on vendor_id). Select DISTINCT vendor_id, vendor_name, and unit_price from these tables to avoid duplicate rows. If primary vendor information is available in MTL_SYSTEM_ITEMS_B, populate primary_supplier_id and primary_supplier_name; otherwise set them to null. Populate the `suppliers` list with each unique supplier record including supplier_id (vendor_id), supplier_name (vendor_name), unit_price, and lead_time_days.
